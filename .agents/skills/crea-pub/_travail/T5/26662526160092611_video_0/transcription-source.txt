The fabric, totally waterproof. The straps, cut-resistant. Three separate compartments for simple and quick organization. We tested it in Rome, in Paris, in Amsterdam, in crowded subways, tourist alleys, the worst places. Zero successful theft attempts. The adjustable strap adapts to all body types.
